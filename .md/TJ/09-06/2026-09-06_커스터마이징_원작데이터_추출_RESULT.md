## 4. 데이터 형식

### 규칙 오브젝트 (`efdata_charactercustomizingrule_N`)

`characterclass`, `strcustomizingrulename`(`EFDLChar_PC_FT.PC_FT`), `faceactionanimset`, `bonemorphanimset`, `arrbonemorphtarget[18].sequencename`, `arrbonemorphanimdata[18]{seqname, rawanimdata[track]{poskeys, rotkeys(quat 16B), scalekeys}}`, `arrmeshmorphrulearray[39..42].morphname`, `arrcustompartitemlook[{strpartitemtype, arrpartitemlook[{strpartitemlook, gameicon}]}]`, `arrcostumedataset`, `arrmaterialexpressionparamgroup[{groupname, arrscalarparameter[{expressionparametername, fdefault, interpolationrange}], arrvectorparameter, arrtextureparameter, arrboolparameter}]`.

FT 슬라이더 18개: cheek_pull, cheekbone_height, cheekbone_pull, eye_angle, eye_height, eye_scaleh, eye_scalev, eye_width, eyebrow_angle, eyebrow_height, jaw_angle, jaw_height, jaw_pull, mouthlip_height, nose_height, nose_length, nose_scale, jaw_scale. 프리셋의 `fmorphweightfactor` 기본값은 0.5다.

### 프리셋 (`efdata_charactercustomizing_N`)

`strcustomizingname`, `arrcustompartitem`(머리카락류 등), `arrbonemorphweight[18]{sequencename, fmorphweightfactor}`, `arrmeshmorphweight[{morphname, fmorphweightfactor}]`, `arrmaterialexpressionparamgroupfactor[{groupname, arrscalarparameterfactor, arrvectorparameterfactor}]`, `busefacepreset`.

### 체형 SkelControl

animtree `skelcontrollists[{bonename, controlhead}]`: b_root→root, bip001-spine→spine0, spine1(+`nextcontrol` spine1_height translation z=20), spine2, l/r clavicle, l/r upperarm, head, neck. 각 `efskelcontrolsingleboneonlyscale{controlname, controlstrength 0.5, strengthtarget, bonescale 2.0}`. `skelcontrolsinglebone` 3개는 spine1_height(0,0,20), l_thigh(0,5,0), r_thigh(0,-5,0) translation(actor space). 기본 프리셋 값은 0.5, thigh/spine1_height는 0.

### MorphTarget raw (`rawdump/VC2N.../<idx>_mm_*.bin`)

`int32 NetIndex`, `FName None`(8B), `int32 lodCount=1`, LOD마다 `int32 count` + `count × {float3 positionDelta, uint32 packedNormal, uint16 sourceIdx, uint16 pad}` + `int32 numBaseMeshVerts`. 45개 파일 전부 `(size-24)/20`이 정수다. `sourceIdx`는 `pc_ft_00_face_sk` LOD0 정점 순서다.

## 7. 추가 실측 (슬라이더 시퀀스)

- `add_*_ui` 시퀀스는 additive 델타다. 3프레임 중 1번이 항등(pos 0, quat 항등)이고 0·2번이 양끝이며 비대칭이다(예: nose_length 0번 z+0.233cm, 2번 y-0.033/z-0.057). 스케일 키는 전부 1.
- 슬라이더별 영향 본: cheek_pull→r/l_cheek_d_cm, cheekbone_*→r/l_cheek_u_cm, eye_angle→gla_cm·earall_r/l_cm(회전), eye_height→gla·earall(이동), eye_scaleh→r/l_eye_l/r_cm, eye_scalev→eye up/dn/l/r 8개, eye_width→earall, eyebrow_*→brow l/c/r 6개+gla, jaw_angle→nose 4+downskel+jaw 5+mouse 2, jaw_height→jaw 5, jaw_pull→nose 4+gla+downskel, mouthlip_height→mouse_dt/ut, nose_height→nose 4+gla, nose_length→nose 4, nose_scale→nose 4, jaw_scale→jaw 5. 표정 `add_fc_smile/angry/cry`도 같은 형식.
- PSA 키 인덱스는 `(firstFrame + frame) * boneCount + bone`이다. `firstraw`는 프레임 번호다.
